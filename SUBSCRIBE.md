# Subscribe to the Authority Trace newsletter

The newsletter is invitation only.

1. Email charak1987@gmail.com with your name, your role, and one line about why you are interested.
2. If your request is approved, you will receive an invitation to subscribe.
3. Open the invitation and sign up at [authoritytrace.substack.com](https://authoritytrace.substack.com/).

Published issues are listed in [NEWSLETTER.md](NEWSLETTER.md).
